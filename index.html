Questions

Before you decide

Does my loved one need to learn anything?

No. The board is always on and they just talk to it. There are no passwords or menus on their side. Setup on their iPad is a one-time code that you enter yourself.

What if she asks the same question many times?

That is what Cassi is for. The same patient answer, every time, without a sigh and without a “like I said.” Repeated questions are normal with cognitive decline, and answering them kindly is the job.

What about privacy? Is it always listening?

A conversation happens only when your loved one speaks to Cassi. Recordings are used only to make Compasse work, and the speech partners that power Cassi are named in our privacy policy, which is written in plain English and covers all of it. Payments are handled by Stripe, so your card number never touches our servers.

Is this a medical device? Can it call 911?

No. Compasse is a comfort and communication tool, not a medical device, an emergency response system, or a monitoring service. It does not call 911, detect falls, or guarantee that medication gets taken. It gives your loved one a calm, familiar presence and gives you a clearer picture of the day. If your family needs emergency response or medical monitoring, please use a dedicated service alongside Compasse, and always keep your existing emergency plans in place.

Does it replace visits and calls?

No, and it never should. Compasse is company between your visits and calls, not a substitute for them. Many families use the board to feel closer: a good morning in your voice, a photo of the day, a call she can start by asking.

What do I need to run Compasse?

An iPad from 2017 or newer for your loved one, and an iPhone for you. The iPad sitting in a drawer somewhere is probably perfect. A simple stand helps, and it should stay plugged in.

Can more than one family member use it?

Right now each family has one caregiver phone connected. Support for siblings and other family members is on the way.

What does it cost?

During our family trial, Compasse is free. At launch it will be $29 a month with the first week free, everything included, cancel anytime. No tiers, no setup fee, no contract, and nothing else to buy.

We will help you close out and delete your family’s data completely.
